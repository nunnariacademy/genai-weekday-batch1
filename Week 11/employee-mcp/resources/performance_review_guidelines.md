# Performance Review Guidelines (Simulated Company Policy)

## Rating scale
- 4.5 - 5.0  Outstanding: consistently exceeds goals, mentors others, drives company-level impact.
- 4.0 - 4.4  Exceeds expectations: delivers beyond scope, reliable owner of outcomes.
- 3.5 - 3.9  Meets expectations: solid delivery, some growth areas identified.
- 3.0 - 3.4  Developing: meets basic goals, needs coaching; typical in the first year.
- Below 3.0  Needs improvement: a 90-day performance improvement plan (PIP) is required.

## What managers evaluate
1. Delivery: quality and timeliness of assigned project work.
2. Collaboration: communication with the team and stakeholders.
3. Ownership: initiative, problem solving, accountability.
4. Growth: learning new skills and applying feedback.

## Review process
- Reviews happen twice a year (June and December).
- Every review must cite at least two concrete examples of the employee's work.
- Raises are linked to rating: 4.5+ eligible for 15-20%, 4.0-4.4 for 8-12%, 3.5-3.9 for 4-7%, below 3.5 for cost-of-living adjustment only.
- Promotion requires two consecutive ratings of 4.0 or higher.
